# Junteng Liu (刘峻腾)

I am a first-year **Ph.D. Candidate** at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://nlp.hkust.edu.hk/), Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, advised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/).

## Research Focus
My research centers on **natural language processing and machine learning**, with particular interests in:
- 🧠 **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- 👁️ **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- ✅ **LLM truthfulness and Interpretability**
